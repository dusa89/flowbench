About FlowBench

FlowBench publishes automation playbooks for small shops — contractors, dental practices, real estate teams, trades businesses — that are losing money to a specific, nameable leak: the missed call, the silent estimate, the slow lead response, the after-hours emergency that goes to a competitor.

Each playbook is a complete operating system for that leak: the sequences, the scripts, the build-vs-buy math, and the legal hard constraints (consent capture, HIPAA gates, call-recording rules) that most automation content skips.

How a playbook gets made

  • Demand first. A playbook topic only exists if we can document, with named sources and dates, that the leak costs real businesses real money. No documented demand, no playbook.
  • Every figure carries a source and a date. Tool prices and program terms are checked against the vendors' own pages. Vendors change tiers often — the check date is printed on the page, and we tell you to verify before buying.
  • The hard constraints are part of the system. AI voice calls need consent captured at quote time. Dental AI needs BAA-signed components. Some states require two-party call-recording announcements. These are printed as required boxes, not footnotes.
  • Rankings are not for sale. Commissions never move a recommendation. Full disclosure policy.
