Affiliate Disclosure

Effective: October 2, 2026

The short version. Tool links on this site are currently affiliate placeholders. When we join a partner program, links become affiliate links: if you click one and sign up for a paid plan, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you.

What this means in practice

  • We intend to participate in the affiliate programs of the automation tools we cover, including but not limited to Make, n8n, Synthflow, and ElevenLabs.
  • A commission is typically a percentage of your subscription, paid by the vendor — your price is identical whether you use our link or go direct.
  • Commissions are how this site funds itself. There is no other revenue: no sponsored placements, no paid reviews, no advertising.
  • Program terms below were checked against each vendor's own affiliate page on October 1-2, 2026. They can change — the vendors' pages are the authority.

Program terms we verified (October 1-2, 2026)

  • Make — 35% commission for 12 months per referral (make.com/en/affiliate).
  • n8n — 30% commission on n8n Cloud referrals for the first year; application/approval required (n8n.io/affiliates).
  • Synthflow — 30% referral commission, 15-month limit on commissions (official changelog).
  • ElevenLabs — 22% of payments for the first 12 months on Starter/Creator/Pro/Scale plans, 11% on Business plans; ~3-month payout hold via PartnerStack; no branded-keyword bidding, no cookie stuffing (elevenlabs.io/affiliates, terms last updated June 10, 2026).

What it doesn't change

  • Rankings are not for sale. No vendor can pay for a better position, a softer criticism, or removal of a constraint we flagged.
  • We recommend tools we don't earn from when they're the right pick — and say so. Zapier appears as a benchmark in our comparisons because it has no public affiliate program.
  • Pricing tables are built from public pricing pages, not from affiliate dashboards. A higher commission never buys a lower shown price.

FTC compliance

This disclosure exists to comply with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255): any material connection between us and a vendor — including affiliate commissions — must be clearly disclosed. You'll find a short disclosure notice on every page containing tool links, linking back to this page.
